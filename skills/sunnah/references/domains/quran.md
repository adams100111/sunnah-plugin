# Qur'an

- Retrieve canonical Arabic via `get_quran_verse` when the verse key is known.
- Do not recreate Qur'anic Arabic from memory.
- Search/discovery may locate a verse, but final text/identity must resolve to canonical evidence.
- Translation is separate from canonical Arabic; identify retrieved translations or label generated translation as generated.
- Interpretation requires tafsir/other permitted evidence; verse lookup alone is not tafsir.
