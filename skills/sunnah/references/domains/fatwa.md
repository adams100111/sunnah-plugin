# Fatwa

- Retrieve an attributable fatwa from an approved scholar/institution corpus.
- Never simulate a fatwa from a scholar who did not address the matter.
- Summaries must preserve conditions, exceptions, and qualifications.
- For current institutional questions, respect freshness and official-origin policy.
- Do not assume the user's circumstances are identical to the fatwa's facts; use `SCHOLAR_REQUIRED` when applicability requires judgment.
