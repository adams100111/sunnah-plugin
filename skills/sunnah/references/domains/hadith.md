# Hadith

- Collections and grading corpora are sources, not simulated personalities.
- Retrieve hadith wording rather than generating canonical text from memory.
- Preserve collection/identifier/narrator/grading attribution when available.
- Report grading as attributed to scholars/sources; do not invent a new grading.
- Preserve material matn/isnad variants and grading disagreement.
- Hadith retrieval does not by itself determine a fiqh ruling.
