# Fiqh

- Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali are methodology lenses, not agent personas.
- Distinguish direct imam statements, transmitted school positions, relied-upon positions, later preference, and contemporary fatwa where evidence allows.
- Without an explicit lens, expose material recognized disagreement rather than silently defaulting to one school.
- A general ruling is not proof that a user's personal circumstances satisfy its conditions.
- Do not perform unrestricted novel ijtihad.
