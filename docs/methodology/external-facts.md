# External Facts

## Principle

Contemporary Islamic questions may depend on facts outside the Islamic corpus.

Examples include:

- medicine;
- financial products;
- contracts;
- regulations;
- technology;
- cryptocurrencies;
- food ingredients;
- AI systems.

The architecture distinguishes:

```text
EXTERNAL FACTUAL EVIDENCE
            ≠
ISLAMIC AUTHORITY EVIDENCE
```

## Responsibilities

External factual evidence may establish:

- what a product does;
- how a contract is structured;
- what a medicine contains;
- how a technology behaves;
- what a regulation currently requires.

It may not by itself establish:

- halal/haram;
- validity of worship;
- legal obligation;
- juristic classification.

## Controlled bridge

A contemporary workflow may look like:

```text
external factual research
        ↓
structured fact model
        ↓
Islamic evidence retrieval
        ↓
methodology-aware synthesis
        ↓
publication gate
```

## Source policy

External factual research may use a broader source universe than Islamic authority retrieval, subject to security and quality rules.

Provenance must remain distinct so the final answer can identify which claims are factual and which are religious conclusions.
