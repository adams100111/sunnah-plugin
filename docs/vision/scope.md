# Scope

## Religious scope

Sunnah Plugin is an **Ahl al-Sunnah wa'l-Jama'ah** knowledge product.

It should represent recognized Sunni scholarship faithfully and preserve legitimate disagreement rather than forcing one synthetic "Sunni answer."

## Target knowledge domains

- Qur'an;
- tafsir;
- hadith and hadith sciences;
- fiqh;
- usul al-fiqh;
- Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali scholarship;
- aqeedah;
- seerah;
- Islamic history;
- biographies and tarajim;
- adhkar;
- worship;
- manners and ethics;
- fatwa retrieval and summarization;
- contemporary Islamic questions;
- Arabic terminology and linguistic context;
- comparative Sunni scholarship.

## Target task types

The single public Sunnah Skill may route to procedures for:

- answer;
- explain;
- summarize;
- compare;
- research;
- verify a claim;
- verify a quotation or attribution;
- inspect a user-provided URL;
- retrieve a scholar or madhhab position;
- retrieve and summarize a fatwa;
- evaluate a contemporary issue using both external facts and Islamic evidence.

## Out of scope

Sunnah Plugin does not own:

- a large internal corpus;
- persistent scholarly knowledge promotion;
- corpus ingestion pipelines at engine scale;
- scholar administration;
- a knowledge graph;
- a database-backed source management platform;
- autonomous novel ijtihad;
- counterfactual scholar simulation.

Those boundaries are intentional. See [Sunnah Engine Boundary](../architecture/sunnah-engine-boundary.md).

## Future optional surface

A lightweight Source Studio may later make Git-backed source configuration easier to inspect and edit. It remains a repository editor and PR surface, not a new source of truth.
