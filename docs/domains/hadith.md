# Hadith Domain

## Responsibility

The hadith procedure supports retrieval, attribution, takhrij-oriented lookup, variant awareness, grading attribution, and grounded explanation.

## Collections are sources

Hadith collections are corpora, not simulated scholar agents.

The system should retrieve from approved collections and metadata rather than asking a model to recreate hadith text from memory.

## Canonical text

When quoting a hadith, use retrieved approved source text.

Preserve available:

- collection;
- book/chapter;
- canonical or local identifier;
- Arabic matn;
- narrator metadata;
- grading attribution;
- source URL or locator.

## Grading

Do not silently create an independent hadith grading from model judgment.

Report attributed grading where available. If grading is disputed, preserve the disagreement.

## Variants

Where material, different wordings or chains should not be collapsed into one synthetic quotation.

## Fiqh boundary

Hadith retrieval or authenticity does not itself establish a legal ruling.

The fiqh procedure determines legal use within the relevant methodology or lens.
