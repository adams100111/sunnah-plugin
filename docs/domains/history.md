# History Domain

## Responsibility

The history procedure retrieves and explains Islamic historical people, events, reports, chronology, and context while preserving source reliability.

## Evidence status

Historical reports may be:

- strongly established;
- multiply transmitted but not canonical;
- disputed;
- late;
- source-dependent;
- unresolved.

The plugin should avoid false precision where historical evidence is genuinely uncertain.

## Attribution

Distinguish:

- primary or early report;
- later historian;
- modern scholarly synthesis;
- generated narrative summary.

## Religious conclusions

Historical reporting does not automatically establish legal or theological authority.

If a historical question becomes a fiqh or aqeedah question, route to the relevant domain procedure.
