# Tafsir Domain

## Responsibility

The tafsir procedure explains Qur'anic passages through approved tafsir evidence and supporting sources where relevant.

## Evidence classes

A tafsir answer may draw on:

- Qur'an;
- authentic or approved hadith evidence;
- asbab al-nuzul reports;
- Arabic language evidence;
- qira'at information;
- classical or contemporary tafsir works;
- attributed scholarly statements.

Each retains its own provenance and status.

## Attribution

Do not flatten multiple mufassir positions into a single anonymous explanation when the differences matter.

A generated synthesis must remain distinguishable from a direct tafsir attribution.

## Historical material

Narrative details appearing in tafsir may have different evidentiary status.

The output should not promote Isra'iliyyat or weak historical reports to Qur'anic or authentic-hadith certainty.

## Qur'an boundary

Canonical Qur'anic text comes from the Qur'an domain or canonical source layer.

The tafsir procedure explains; it does not recreate canonical scripture.
