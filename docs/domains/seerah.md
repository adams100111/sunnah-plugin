# Seerah Domain

## Responsibility

The seerah procedure answers questions about the life of the Prophet ﷺ while preserving the evidentiary status of narrative details.

## Evidence hierarchy

Relevant material may include:

- Qur'an;
- authentic hadith;
- athar;
- early seerah reports;
- later historical works;
- scholarly reconstruction.

These are not equal in evidentiary strength.

## Narrative quality

The product should still tell coherent, useful stories.

Evidence classification should improve the narrative rather than reduce every answer to a list of caveats.

Where a popular detail is weak or uncertain, distinguish it naturally from what is firmly established.

## Legal boundary

A historical action or report does not automatically establish a contemporary legal ruling.

Questions that ask both "what happened?" and "what should I do?" require cross-domain fiqh or usul handling.
