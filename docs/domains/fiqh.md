# Fiqh Domain

## Responsibility

The fiqh procedure retrieves and explains recognized Sunni juristic positions without inventing rulings or collapsing madhhab methodology.

## Madhhab lenses

The four major Sunni madhhabs are first-class lenses:

- Hanafi;
- Maliki;
- Shafi'i;
- Hanbali.

A comparative request may activate several lenses.

## Attribution levels

Where evidence permits, distinguish:

- direct statement from an imam;
- narration from an imam;
- position transmitted within the madhhab;
- relied-upon or mu'tamad position;
- later scholar preference;
- contemporary fatwa.

These must not be represented as interchangeable.

## Default behavior

When the user does not select a lens and recognized disagreement is material, expose the disagreement.

Do not silently default all Sunni questions to Hanbali or Saudi contemporary positions.

## Applicability

Separate the retrieved general ruling from whether the user's situation satisfies its conditions.

Where personal applicability requires judgment not established by evidence, provide the sourced general rule and use `SCHOLAR_REQUIRED` where appropriate.

## Evidence

Fiqh evidence may depend on Qur'an, hadith, usul, madhhab transmission, and scholar or institutional fatwa material.

The plugin should request only the dependencies needed by the issue.

## No novel ijtihad

The plugin may compare and faithfully synthesize transmitted positions.

It is not authorized to perform unrestricted novel ijtihad.
