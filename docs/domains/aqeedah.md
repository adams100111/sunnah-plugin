# Aqeedah Domain

## Responsibility

The aqeedah procedure answers Sunni creed and theology questions using approved sources and explicit attribution.

## Source policy

Aqeedah is sensitive to terminology and school or context.

The active source policy must define which works, scholars, and institutional sources are allowed for particular claim classes.

## Attribution

Avoid vague statements such as "Islam says" when the evidence actually supports:

- a Qur'anic statement;
- a hadith statement;
- a named scholar;
- a named Sunni theological formulation;
- a later explanatory summary.

## Disagreement

Recognized Sunni differences in formulation or interpretation should be exposed when material.

Do not manufacture agreement by stripping terminology from its scholarly context.

## Generated synthesis

A synthesis may organize approved material for clarity, but each substantive theological claim remains grounded and attributable.
